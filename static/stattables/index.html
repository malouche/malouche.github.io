Statistical Distribution Tables

An interactive replacement for the Z, t, χ2 and F tables at the back of a statistics textbook. Enter any cutoff to obtain the tail probability, or any significance level to obtain the corresponding critical value. Each query is paired with a shaded density plot so that the numerical answer is always visualised.
