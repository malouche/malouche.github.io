abstract: >
  RAS-pathway inhibitors are entering pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma (PDAC)
  trials at pace, yet the preclinical evidence supporting them is heterogeneous
  in both the KRAS allele studied and the degree of model validation. This
  systematic review synthesises preclinical evidence on allele-stratified
  activity of RAS inhibitors in PDAC, stratifying the synthesis by the level of
  model validation rather than pooling validated and cell-line-only evidence
  together, and complements it with an exploratory meta-analysis of early-phase
  clinical trials. Separating rather than excluding weakly validated evidence
  makes the dependence of apparent efficacy on model provenance explicit and
  clarifies which allele-specific signals are currently supported by
  transplantable or genetically engineered models.
