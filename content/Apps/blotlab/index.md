**BlotLab** is a guided Western blot for the teaching laboratory. It follows the wet-transfer method in eight steps, from casting the gel to quantifying the bands, with a checklist for each step (kept on the user's own device), built-in timers, and two calculators for the quantities the protocol needs: a gel recipe calculator and a sample loading calculator.

## Why it is on this page

Western blotting is often taught as a recipe. BlotLab treats it as quantitative work as well: recipe volumes and loading amounts are calculated, and the last step ends in band quantification. It is a teaching tool, and the app reminds users that times, voltages and recipes are typical starting points that give way to the protocol of their own laboratory.

## Data and licence
