**MizanLab** collects the everyday arithmetic of the chemistry and biology bench in five calculators: a concentration converter (M, mM, µM, nM, mg/mL, µg/mL and more), molar mass from a chemical formula with the breakdown by element, percent solutions (w/v, v/v and w/w, to prepare or to dilute), buffer and media recipes scaled to the volume needed, and centrifugation, converting rpm to relative centrifugal force and back. Every answer comes with the formula and the full working, so the student sees why the number is what it is.

MizanLab was first released as ChemCalc, whose old address now points to it. Cell seeding and serial dilutions are in its companion app, CellCalc.

## Why it is on this page

Unit errors are among the commonest mistakes at the bench. By showing the formula and carrying the units through each step, the calculators serve as worked examples of quantitative reasoning, and the app reminds users to check critical calculations against their own protocol.

## Data and licence
