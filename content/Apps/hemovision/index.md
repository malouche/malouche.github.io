**HemoVision Pro** is a virtual haemocytometer. The student taps to place live and dead cells on the counting grid, chooses the large or the small square, enters the dilution factor, the culture volume and the target density, and reads off the viability, the cell density and the volume needed to subculture at the target density. A panel beside the grid explains each step in words: why total cells divided by target density gives a volume, where the factor of 10,000 comes from, and how well the sample was mixed, judged by the coefficient of variation of the counts across squares. A heatmap and a demo mode make the pattern easier to see.

## Why it is on this page

A cell count is a small statistical experiment: a sample of squares, a mean, a dilution factor and a check on whether the sample is representative. HemoVision Pro lets students see that chain of reasoning before they apply it at the microscope.
